Agentic Device Trust Mesh: executive summary

Every customer device that touches a financial account (a phone, a tablet or a browser) is either trusted or a risk. This use case shows how a bank can manage that trust in real time with a mesh of cooperating AI agents built on Kafka, Flink and KubeRay. Each agent has one narrow job: enrolment, risk scoring, step-up checks, revocation or compliance. Together they replace rigid, batch-driven device policy with continuous, explainable decisions.
